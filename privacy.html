Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Ketsy Online ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data when you visit our website at ketsyonline.com or contact us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number when you submit our contact form or book a strategy call
  • Details about your business or project shared in messages
  • Payment information when you engage our services (processed securely through our payment provider — we do not store card numbers)

We also collect certain information automatically when you visit our site:

  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent on site, and referring URLs
  • IP address (anonymised where required)

2. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking tools, including:

  • Google Analytics 4 — to understand how visitors use our site (aggregated, anonymised data)
  • Meta Pixel — to measure the effectiveness of our advertising on Facebook and Instagram
  • Google Tag Manager — to manage the deployment of the above tags

You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. You can manage Meta ad preferences at facebook.com/ads/preferences.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries and fulfil service agreements
  • To send you updates relevant to your project (you may opt out at any time)
  • To improve our website and marketing based on aggregated analytics
  • To comply with our legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

4. How We Share Your Information

We share data only with trusted service providers who help us operate our business, including:

  • Calendly — for booking strategy calls
  • Formsubmit.co — for processing contact form submissions
  • Google LLC — for Analytics
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. — for advertising measurement

All third-party providers are required to handle your data securely and in accordance with applicable law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your contact information for as long as necessary to deliver our services and meet legal requirements. Enquiry data from non-clients is typically deleted within 12 months of last contact.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email us at ketsyonline@gmail.com.

7. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
